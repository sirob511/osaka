Osaka Garden Chicago — evergreen content policy
Editorial decision: November 18, 2026

Purpose
Keep the guide useful between visits and seasons. Explain the garden, its
history, typical seasonal patterns, recurring gatherings, and practical
orientation. Let the organizations responsible for the garden and its events
publish changing operational details.

Visitor-facing copy
- Use general seasonal guidance and qualify variable bloom timing.
- Link to official Park District information for hours, closures, access,
  and current blossom conditions.
- Link to organizer calendars or evergreen event pages for dates, programs,
  registration, capacity, and event-specific participation instructions.
- Do not repeat a past edition's date, hours, registration requirement, or
  capacity as a summary of what a visitor should expect next.
- Avoid dated trackers, annual placeholder pages, “last listed” summaries,
  and unannounced-next-year notices. Do not claim live reporting.
- Preserve historical years when they explain the garden, artwork, or
  origins of a tradition. Historical facts are distinct from event listings.
- Keep links descriptive so visitors know where to find updates. A dated
  source can still support a historical fact; it should not be presented as
  the source for current scheduling or visitor instructions.

Maintenance records
Retain research dates, source notes, image provenance, and metadata needed
for editorial maintenance. They do not need to become visible status
messages throughout the site. Check changing source links when editing the
relevant page, and favor the organization responsible for the information.

Retired pages
The former /cherry-blossoms/2027/ tracker permanently redirects to
/cherry-blossoms/#bloom-updates. It is no longer generated, linked in site
navigation, or included in the sitemap. Vercel and the local preview server
share redirect definitions in vercel.json. Other static hosts need equivalent
redirect configuration.

The older /updates/ redirect to /history/#garden-plans remains in place.

Update destinations verified for this change
- Lantern ceremony: https://daiyuzenji.org/special-events and the Park District event calendar. The JAF festival archive still showed older listings, so it is not the primary updates destination.
- Earth Day: https://www.chicagoparkdistrict.com/earth-day replaces the old individual Jackson Park event listing.
- Hours: https://www.chicagoparkdistrict.com/parks-facilities/osaka-garden
- Bloom reports and access: https://www.chicagoparkdistrict.com/cherry-blossoms
